Technology & Digital Media Newsletter

October 11, 2026 • By Lucas Korzeniewski

Building a foundation in technology

Technology has interested me since I was young, and studying Computer Information Technology at State College of Florida gives me a way to develop that interest further. Learning about computers involves understanding how different parts of a system work together. When a problem occurs, it helps to identify the symptoms and work through possible causes one at a time. Developing these habits is an important part of preparing for a career in IT.

Connecting technical skills with creative work

Digital media brings together my interest in computers and my experience with creative software. My certifications in Adobe Premiere Pro and Adobe Photoshop have given me a foundation in video and image editing. Both kinds of work require attention to detail, from choosing an image size to arranging clips in a sequence. As I continue learning, I want to build on these skills and better understand how technology supports creative projects.

Music as a source of inspiration

Listening to music and collecting vinyl are two of my favorite interests outside of school. A record combines sound with visual design through its cover, packaging, and printed information. That connection makes music an interesting starting point for thinking about digital media. Whether I am looking at album artwork or exploring a website, I enjoy noticing how design choices help communicate an idea and shape the experience.

 Technology & creative practice 

  • Approach computer problems by checking one possible cause at a time.
  • Use image and video editing skills to communicate ideas clearly.
  • Look at album artwork for examples of color, typography, and composition.
